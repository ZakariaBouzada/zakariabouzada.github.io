Master's Thesis 2025

Opinionated IaC Bundles:
The Future of SME Cloud

An investigation into reducing deployment effort and technical barriers for Small and Medium Enterprises through reusable automation framework models.

Zakaria Bouzada | Åbo Akademi University

The SME "Complexity Gap"

The Problem

  • Small & Medium Enterprises lack dedicated DevOps departments.
  • Standard Cloud consoles present too many options, risking secure setups.
  • Raw IaC tools require highly specialized engineering talent.

The Solution

  • Opinionated Frameworks: Making design decisions ahead of time on behalf of the user.
  • Pre-packaging security, structure, and networking basics dynamically.

The Multi-Cloud Bundle Framework

A progressive abstraction architecture mapping business needs directly to automated multi-cloud environments.

Framework Flexibility & Portability

  • Customizable Scope: Bundles A, B, and C represent specific SME models selected for this thesis, but the underlying engine can be tailored to deploy any target architecture — including virtual machines (VMs), database clusters, serverless logic, or security nodes.
  • Provider Agnostic: Because the blueprints rely on Terraform, every single bundle is fundamentally reusable across AWS, GCP, Microsoft Azure, and any other provider infrastructure platform.
